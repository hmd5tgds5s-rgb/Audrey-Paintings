    About Audrey

    Audrey paints animals with personality: a cockatoo mid-ruffle, a fawn tucked into the ferns, a frog sizing you up from the pond. She works in acrylic, building color in loose, layered strokes so every feather and ripple keeps the energy of the brush.

    Her work ranges from tiny canvases that fit in your palm to large statement pieces. When she isn't painting wildlife, she's painting someone's beloved pet.

    Questions

    Buying, paying, commissions and care.

    How do I buy a print or an original?

    Send the form above (or email Audrey) with the painting you like. She'll confirm sizes, price and shipping by email, and you pay her directly. Prints are the main offering for now; ask if you're hoping for an original.

    How do I pay?

    Payment is arranged by email and made directly to Audrey by Venmo, Apple Cash, Cash App or Zelle. Nothing is charged through this website.

    How long does a commission take?

    Most portraits take two to four weeks once you approve the sketch. Larger canvases can take longer. Audrey confirms the timeline when she replies.

    How do I send photos for a commission?

    After you send the form, Audrey emails you back. Reply to that email with three or four clear, well-lit photos, ideally taken at your animal's eye level.

    Do paintings come ready to hang?

    Stretched canvases arrive ready to hang with painted edges, so they look finished without a frame. Small canvas panels and minis look best in a small frame or on a tabletop easel.

    Where do you ship?

    Within the US for now. Audrey confirms the shipping cost by email before you pay.

    How do I care for my painting?

    Hang it out of direct sunlight and away from humid spots like bathrooms. Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth. No water or cleaners needed.
